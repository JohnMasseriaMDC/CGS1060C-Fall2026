Paste is really two buttons

The top half pastes right away. The arrow underneath asks how to paste, which matters when you copy from a website or another document.

OptionWhat you get
Keep Source FormattingText looks like it did where you copied it. This is what the top half does.
Merge FormattingText adopts the look of where you paste it, but keeps basics like bold.
Keep Text OnlyPlain text, no formatting at all. Best for pasting from the web.
PictureA single image of the text. You can't edit it afterward.

The Clipboard pane keeps your last 24 cut or copied items, from any Office app.

  • Open it with the small launcher arrow in the Clipboard group
  • Click any item to paste it, in any order
  • Paste All and Clear All work on everything at once
Copy (Ctrl+C) leaves the original. Cut (Ctrl+X) removes it. Both land on the Clipboard.

Replace All is powerful and a little dangerous

Replace changes one match at a time. Replace All changes every match, including ones hidden inside other words.

Replace “cat” with “dog” → Replace All
The dog slept on the spa dogalog while staff completed their edudogion.

Fix: click More >> and check Find whole words only. Add Match case when capitals matter.

It can find what you can't type. Under More >> › Special:

Styles: format once, change everywhere

A style is a saved bundle of formatting with a name. Manual formatting gives you the same look, but none of the benefits.

  • Change the style, and every paragraph using it updates at once
  • Heading styles show up in the Navigation pane as a clickable outline
  • A table of contents builds itself from heading styles
  • Changing the document theme restyles everything consistently
Where: Home › Styles gallery. Right-click a style › Modify to change it everywhere.

Indents live on the ruler

Turn it on at View › Ruler. The markers on the left edge are three separate controls stacked together.

MarkerMoves
▽ Top triangleFirst line only
△ Bottom triangleEvery line except the first (hanging)
▭ RectangleBoth together (left indent)

Exact amounts: Layout › Paragraph › Indent Left or Right. Type a value and press Enter.

For hanging indents, use Paragraph dialog › Special › Hanging. Never use Enter and Tab on each line.

Tab stops, not the spacebar

Each tab stop tells text where to go when you press Tab, and how to line up once it gets there.

Select the lines first. Tab stops belong to paragraphs, so they only apply where your selection or cursor is. Change type with the selector at the ruler's far left; drag a stop off the ruler to delete it.

Leaders fill the gap

The Tabs dialog sets a stop at an exact position and can add a leader: dots, dashes, or a line that fills the tabbed space.

Why did my list start over at 1?

Any paragraph that isn't numbered breaks the list. Word guesses you're starting a new one, and you can tell it otherwise.

1.  Check in at the front desk¶
2.  Change into a robe¶
Lockers are available near the pool.¶
1.  Wait in the relaxation lounge¶
2.  Your therapist will meet you¶

Should be 3 and 4

Apply Numbering to the second group, then click the AutoCorrect Options smart tag that appears beside it:

  • Continue Numbering picks up where the last list stopped
  • After that, the same tag offers Restart Numbering

The tag disappears once you click elsewhere. If you miss it, right-click the first number for the same choices.

Quick check

Type your answer in the chat, then click a card to reveal it.

Your cursor is blinking inside a paragraph with nothing selected. You click Center. What gets centered?

The whole paragraph. Alignment is paragraph formatting, so the cursor is enough.

SimNet says “click the Shading button arrow.” You click the paint bucket itself. What happens?

Word applies the last shading color used instead of opening the palette. The arrow opens the choices.

You pasted a quote from a website and it came in with a gray background and a strange font. Which paste option should you have used?

Keep Text Only. It drops all formatting from the source.

Your works-cited entries need every line indented except the first. What is that called, and where do you set it?

A hanging indent. Paragraph dialog › Special › Hanging, the bottom triangle on the ruler, or Ctrl+T.

You set a decimal tab stop, but only the first line of your price list lines up. What happened?

Only that paragraph was selected. Select all the lines, then set the tab stop.

Fields: text Word writes for you

Page numbers, dates, document properties, a table of contents, and a bibliography are not typed text. They are fields, and Word keeps them up to date.

Page 7 of 12
Click a field and it turns gray. That's how you know Word owns it.

Fields update in different ways:

  • By themselves: page numbers, property controls
  • On open, if you let them: the date stamp, when Update automatically is checked
  • Only when asked: table of contents, index, bibliography
FieldWhere it comes from
Page numberHeader & Footer tab › Page Number
Date stampInsert › Date & Time
Title, AuthorQuick Parts › Document Property, filled from File › Info
Contents, index, bibliographyReferences tab
Select a field and press F9 to refresh it. Typing over a field replaces it with ordinary text.

Headers and footers are a separate room

The top and bottom margins are their own editing area. What you put there repeats on every page automatically.

  • Get in: double-click the top or bottom margin, or Insert › Header
  • Get out: double-click the body, or click Close Header and Footer
  • Skip the cover page: check Different First Page in the Options group
  • Page numbers: Header & Footer › Page Number. Insert once and every page numbers itself
Never type page numbers by hand. Add a page in the middle and every number after it would be wrong.

Why the picture won't move

A newly inserted picture is In Line with Text. Word treats it as a single, very large character, so it can only go where a letter could go.

Click the picture, then the Layout Options button at its top-right corner.

OptionEffect
In Line with TextStuck in the paragraph. The default
Square / TightText flows around it. Drag it anywhere
Behind TextBecomes a background image
Picture Format › Position applies a spot and a wrapping style in one click.

Resize without squashing, and write alt text

Two small habits that separate a finished document from a homemade one.

Alt text describes the picture for someone using a screen reader, and shows if the image fails to load.

  • Right-click the picture › View Alt Text
  • Write one clear sentence: Therapist applying hot stones to a guest's back
  • Word can generate a description. Read it: they are often wrong
  • Mark as decorative for images that carry no information
SimNet grades alt text, and many workplaces require it. A blank box is a fail either way.

Marking index entries

The courtyard{ XE "courtyard" } is open daily.

Mark Entry hides a code in the document and switches formatting marks on, so the page looks like it fell apart. It didn't.

Mark everything first, then Insert Index. Mark more later and you must click Update Index.

No Markup does not mean no changes

The Display for Review box on the Review tab changes your view only. The tracked edits stay in the file until you accept or reject them.

Display for ReviewYou see
Simple MarkupThe finished text, with a change bar in the margin
All MarkupEvery insertion, deletion, and who made it
No MarkupThe document as if everything were accepted. The changes are still there.
OriginalThe document before any edits
To really remove them: Review › Accept or Reject, one at a time or all at once.

Comments (Ctrl+Alt+M): Reply keeps a conversation together. Resolve grays it out but keeps it. Delete removes it.

Before submitting anything: File › Info › Check for Issues › Inspect Document finds leftover comments and changes.

Quick check: Session 2

Type your answer in the chat, then click a card to reveal it.

You applied a new theme, but three of your headings didn't change. Why not?

They were formatted by hand instead of with heading styles. Themes only reach text using theme fonts and styles.

You added two pages in the middle of your paper. What has to happen before you print?

Update the table of contents, and any index or bibliography. They're fields and don't refresh themselves.

You inserted a picture and it won't move anywhere on the page. What's wrong?

It's In Line with Text. Use Layout Options and pick Square or Tight.

Your instructor can still see every edit you made, even though you set Display for Review to No Markup. Why?

No Markup only hides them. The changes are still in the file until you accept or reject them.
