ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORK
Connect neurons in layers and they can learn
Learning = adjusting weights
  1. Make a guess
  2. Compare with the right answer
  3. Nudge every weight a little
  4. Repeat millions of times
"Deep" learning just means many hidden layers. Today's large models have billions of weights.

FROM DEEP LEARNING TO GENERATIVE AI
Same networks, new job: create, not label
RECOGNIZE · 2010s
Input → a label
Photo of a cat → "cat"
Your voice → "set a timer"
An email → "spam"
GENERATE · 2020s
Prompt → new content
"A poem about my cat" → a new poem
"A cat at the beach" → a new image
"Summarize this" → a new paragraph
Generative AI is an outgrowth of deep learning. The text kind is called a large language model (LLM).

HOW GENERATIVE AI LEARNS LANGUAGE
It learned by guessing the next word
Training, over and over
  1. Take a sentence from books, articles, or websites
  2. Hide the next word and have the model guess it
  3. Check the real word; nudge the weights
  4. Repeat across trillions of words
The cat sat on the ___
Illustrative scores, not real model output
To write an answer, it picks a likely next word, adds it, and repeats, one word at a time.

Real models use hundreds to thousands of numbers per token. Each number is one dimension.
Words with similar meanings get similar vectors, so they point in similar directions.

RECAP
Five ideas to carry into the GenAI lecture
  1. You've used AI for years: Siri, Alexa, Face ID, spam filters.
  2. Machine learning learns from examples instead of rules.
  3. Neural networks borrow the brain's idea: simple units with adjustable connections.
  4. Generative AI is deep learning trained to predict the next word, again and again.
  5. Words become vectors, and similar meanings point in similar directions.
